none at hand). They come from Wikimedia Commons; each is credited on the
page under its thumbnail (author, licence link, source link) and listed
here; keep this table and photos.json in step. Resized to 1280 px on the long
side and saved as WebP without metadata; otherwise unchanged. The list the
page reads is [photos.json](photos.json).
