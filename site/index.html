Pointsmanbuildings demo

Every building, the same checks

These are the buildings of one neighbourhood in Kiyota-ku, Sapporo, from the national 3D city model (PLATEAU). They live in GeonicDB, a FIWARE data platform, as Building entities. Soon you can send a request about one of them, step by step, and watch Pointsman sort it: what to check first, and why.

Pointsman sorts and explains. It never decides that a building is safe: that is always a person's decision.
