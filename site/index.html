How it works

What happens to a request, step by step. GeonicDB, a FIWARE data platform, keeps the data and starts each step. A small connector passes the step to Pointsman, and Pointsman answers. The AI only answers fixed questions about your words; the building’s data and the city’s rules do the rest.

  1. 1 · SavedThe page saves the request in GeonicDB
  2. 2 · NotifiedGeonicDB tells the connector
  3. 3 · First checkPointsman sorts the building
  4. 4 · Your descriptionPointsman reads your words
  5. 5 · Written backThe results go back into GeonicDB
  6. 6 · UsedAny app can read the results

Every building, the same checks

These are the buildings of one neighbourhood in Kiyota-ku, Sapporo, from the national 3D city model (PLATEAU). They live in GeonicDB, a FIWARE data platform, as Building entities. Pick one, ask how urgent a seismic check is, and watch Pointsman sort the request: what to check first, and why.

Pointsman sorts and explains. It never decides that a building is safe: that is always a person's decision.
